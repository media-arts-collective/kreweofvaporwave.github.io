Join the Krewe List

The Krewe talks over email. Two steps, both of them email — there is no form, no account, and no password.

  • 1. Send the blank email →

    Nothing to fill out. Send it from whatever address you want krewe mail at.

  • 2. Reply to the robot.

    Google writes back to check it's really your address. Hit reply and send. That's it — you're on the list.

About that second email: the subject is Join request for kreweofvaporwave trailed by a chunk of gibberish, and it looks exactly like junk mail. Check your spam folder for it. It has a Join This Group button in it — ignore the button, it goes to a page you can't see. Replying is what works.

Once you're in: your first few posts get held for a moderator, which is how we keep the robots out. Add kreweofvaporwave@googlegroups.com to your contacts so the Krewe doesn't land in your spam folder.

Already in? Hand this page to anybody who should be. You don't have to route it through an officer.
