Join the Krewe List

The Krewe talks on a Google Group. Two ways in — either one sends a join request to the moderators.

  • Email to subscribe — send the blank message. Nothing to fill out.
  • Ask to join on the web — needs a Google account.

Watch for a reply. Google writes back to confirm it's really your address. Click the link in that message (or just reply to it) or your request never reaches us.

Then, so the Krewe doesn't land in your spam folder: add kreweofvaporwave@googlegroups.com to your contacts. In Gmail you can also make a filter for that address and check Never send it to Spam.

Already in? Send this page to anybody who should be. You don't need to route it through an officer.
